MEDIEVAL SIEGE

Hold the road. Build towers. Freeze the horde.

Rebel waves march along the road toward your castle. Build Archer, Cannon and Catapult towers to destroy them, and Frost towers to slow them to a crawl. Survive all 20 waves — beware the boss at wave 10 and 20.

  • Place a tower: tap a tower in the bottom bar, then tap a green plot.
  • Upgrade / sell: tap a tower you've built.
  • Abilities: Arrow Storm & Frost Nova (bottom-left) — tap, then tap the battlefield.
  • Camera: drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Call early: start the next wave during prep for bonus gold.
